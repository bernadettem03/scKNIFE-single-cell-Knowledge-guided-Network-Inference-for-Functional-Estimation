Figure 6 | Cross-platform metabolic analysis identifies treatment-associated pathway remodeling, longitudinal inferred-metabolite recovery and a sulfur-handling context in TNBC.

(A) Four-layer Surgery–Biopsy overlap. MEIA contains 218 scCellFie tasks and 138 Human-GEM pathways; GPR-GPA contains the same 138 Human-GEM pathways. Of 22 LC–MS candidate pathways, 18 source terms mapped to 14 unique Human-GEM targets and four remained unmapped. The intended exclusive intersections are 218 MEIA-task features, 124 MEIA-pathway/GPR-GPA-only pathways, 14 pathways shared with mapped LC–MS, and four unmapped LC–MS pathways. Set membership denotes analytical coverage, not significance or directional agreement. (B) LC–MS pathway enrichment for Surgery versus Biopsy. Axes show enrichment factor and −log10(permutation-adjusted Gamma p); point size scales with the displayed pathway-evidence radius. Three of 22 pathways passed pathway BH FDR<0.05: selenoamino acid metabolism (3/6 hits, p=0.00169, q=0.0371), aspartate and asparagine metabolism (4/8, p=0.00204, q=0.0428), and glycolysis/gluconeogenesis (3/9, p=0.00246, q=0.0492). Enrichment is not a directional flux estimate. (C) Pooled matched-score concordance with MEIA activity on x and direct scKNIFE activity on y. The two density plots separately include all 218 tasks (15,127,020 cell–task pairs; Pearson r=0.8981, Spearman ρ=0.9170) and all 138 pathways (9,575,820 cell–pathway pairs; r=0.8934, ρ=0.9684), across the same 69,390 matched cells. Both score matrices retain the existing separate task/pathway compositional normalization. Axes are linear and retain the full observed ranges; colour shows cell–feature pairs per bin on a shared logarithmic count scale. Dotted charcoal lines show ordinary least-squares fits of scKNIFE scores on MEIA scores, computed separately from all matched task or pathway observations. These are descriptive fits; no confidence bands or inferential P values are shown. All observations contribute to the bins and coefficients, without downsampling or axis trimming. These pooled coefficients include between-feature differences and are not typical individual-feature correlations. Shared-input concordance is descriptive technical agreement, not independent biological validation or measured flux.

(D) Two CAF Tier-3 inferred-metabolite programs shown across three contexts. In the six-patient discovery cohort, (2E)-heptenoyl-CoA decreased in 5/6 patients (mean Surgery–Bx Δ=−0.163; exact two-sided sign-flip p=0.0625; BH q₄=0.125), and glutarylcarnitine decreased in 6/6 (Δ=−0.132; p=0.0313; q₄=0.125). In the single longitudinal patient P3, both decreased at Surgery and rebounded at Recurrence: (2E)-heptenoyl-CoA median Bx/Surgery/Recurrence scores were 0.353/0.279/0.331 (Surgery–Bx δ=−0.340, q=5.08×10⁻⁷; Recurrence–Surgery δ=+0.248, q=1.65×10⁻¹³), and glutarylcarnitine scores were 0.360/0.315/0.362 (δ=−0.221, q=0.00169; δ=+0.193, q=1.24×10⁻⁸). Neither node showed a supported discovery-cohort metastatic-status association (mean metastatic–non-metastatic differences +0.021 and +0.006; Cliff’s δ=+0.333 for both; exact p=0.80; BH q₄=0.80). Patients are the replicate for treatment and status tests; P3 cell distributions are descriptive, and inferred activity is neither abundance nor flux. (E) Human-GEM context for 68 unique Tier-3 nodes: fatty-acid oxidation/carnitine handling, 38 (55.9%); no specific pathway, 13 (19.1%); glycoconjugate/sphingolipid remodeling, 10 (14.7%); amino-acid metabolism, 3 (4.4%); glycerophospholipid metabolism, 2 (2.9%); nucleotide/pyrimidine metabolism, 1 (1.5%); glycolysis/gluconeogenesis, 1 (1.5%).

(F) Paired macrophage patient-median MEIA task scores. Homocysteine degradation decreased in 7/7 patients (median Surgery–Bx Δ=−3.05×10⁻⁴; exact two-sided Wilcoxon p=0.0156; BH q₄₅=0.281), and glutathione synthesis decreased in 6/7 (Δ=−2.78×10⁻⁴; p=0.0313; q₄₅=0.281). Excluding P3 retained decreases in 6/6 and 5/6 patients (p=0.0313 and 0.0625). Patients are the biological replicates; neither task passed screen-wide FDR. (G) Paired negative-mode LC–MS feature m/z 215.0260, RT 384.64 s. All seven patients decreased at Surgery (patient-blocked log2FC=−1.858; nominal p=0.00282; joint BH q=0.0636); all 14 specimens were observed without imputation, and median technical-replicate CV was 46.6%. The mass is compatible with KEGG C05692, Se-adenosyl-L-selenohomocysteine, as [M−2H]²⁻, but no native MS/MS match or authentic standard was available; it is an accurate-mass MS1 candidate only. (H) NG8 knowledge-backbone subgraph containing 20 NG8 nodes plus 15 minimum connectors (35 nodes, 42 edges, one connected component). Selenocystathionine is outlined. Graph connectivity supplies biochemical context, not node-level significance, causality or flux evidence.

MEIA, Multilayer Evidence-Integrated Activity; GPR-GPA, gene–protein–reaction-derived gene/protein activity; LC–MS, liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry; Bx, biopsy; BH FDR, Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery rate; NG8, node group 8.
