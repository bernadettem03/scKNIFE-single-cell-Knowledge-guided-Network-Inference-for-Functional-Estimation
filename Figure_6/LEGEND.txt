Figure 6 | Tissue metabolic pathway context and patient-resolved inferred activity after neoadjuvant treatment in TNBC.

Cell compartments in panels d–t use the Figure 4 Wu SingleR major annotations joined by cell ID. Cancer and normal epithelial calls are combined as Epithelial. The six prespecified compartments are CAF, Endothelial, Epithelial, Myeloid, PVL and T cells. B cells and Plasmablasts are retained in a separate eight-group sensitivity screen. Patient, rather than cell, is the unit of paired inference.

(a) Analytical coverage of 21 LC–MS candidate pathways by MEIA and GPR-GPA scores. Eighteen source terms map to 14 unique Human-GEM targets; three are unmapped. The 17 LC–MS display entries comprise the 14 targets plus three unmapped terms. (b) Surgery–biopsy LC–MS pathway enrichment. Five of 21 candidates pass Holm-adjusted Gamma P < 0.05: selenoamino acid metabolism (0.0336), purine metabolism (0.0361), methionine/cysteine metabolism (0.0361), aspartate/asparagine metabolism (0.0389) and glycolysis/gluconeogenesis (0.0441). (c) Descriptive pooled MEIA–scKNIFE task-score concordance from related computational inputs.

(d–k) Inferred scKNIFE node activity in seven matched biopsy–surgery patients using the Figure 4 Wu SingleR major Myeloid group. (d) Selenocystathionine is lower at surgery in 6/7 patients (median paired surgery–biopsy difference −0.0506; exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon nominal P=0.03125). It provides selenium/sulfur biochemical context rather than a direct selenoamino-acid pathway graph match. (e–g) Purine-linked adenine, guanine and deoxyinosine, respectively, are lower in 7/7, 6/7 and 6/7 patients (median differences −0.0477, −0.0645 and −0.0392; nominal P=0.015625, 0.03125 and 0.046875; within-pathway BH q=0.1875, 0.1875 and 0.2540). Across D–K, each paired boxplot shows seven biopsy and surgery patient medians with observed-range whiskers; the same patient colors identify points and connecting lines in every panel. E–G use a shared 0–0.65 inferred node-score axis. (h) Methionine/cysteine-linked L-cystathionine is lower in 7/7 (−0.0319; P=0.015625; q=0.1613). (i–j) Aspartate/asparagine-linked 3-sulfinoalanine and (S)-2-aminobutanoate, respectively, are each lower in 7/7 (−0.0189 and −0.0535; P=0.015625 and q=0.1691 for each). (k) Glycolysis/gluconeogenesis-linked methylglyoxal is lower in 6/7 (−0.0297; P=0.046875; q=0.2720). D–K use the same biopsy/surgery box colors, patient-specific line and point colors, and panel annotation style. Each line joins one patient's two specimen medians. I and J have separate node-score axes. Only the paired biopsy–surgery comparison is displayed. Node–pathway links in E–G and I–K were verified by shared Human-GEM reactions. The screen contains 1,344 unique node–cell tests across six compartments; its smallest global BH q is 0.1757, and none of the displayed nodes passes within-pathway BH q<0.05. P7 has nine Myeloid cells at surgery; six-patient sensitivity results are reported in Supplemental Table 6C. These are post-selection inferred activity comparisons, not measurements of metabolite concentrations or flux.

(l–m) Epithelial cytosolic and endoplasmic-reticular fatty acid activation pathway scores, respectively, are higher at surgery in 7/7 patients (each exact paired Wilcoxon nominal P=0.015625; 828-test pathway-screen BH q=0.5074). (n) The Myeloid peroxisomal carnitine-shuttle pathway score is lower at surgery in 6/7 patients (P=0.046875; pathway-screen q=0.5245). L–N display seven patient-level biopsy and surgery medians per box; thin lines join paired values and orange identifies P3. These three MEIA pathway scores come from the same separately compositional matrix and are exploratory results from the six-compartment, 828-comparison pathway screen.

(o) The frozen Figure 4 H-derived UMAP localizes 6,014 biopsy/surgery Wu-major Myeloid cells colored by inferred L-octanoylcarnitine node activity (2nd–98th percentile scale); grey points show all atlas cells. (p) Seven paired patient medians show lower L-octanoylcarnitine activity at surgery in 7/7 patients (nominal exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon P=0.015625; focused 60-test BH q=0.1442). P1–P7 colors match panels D–K. The focused family comprises ten carnitine graph nodes across six Wu-major compartments. Supplementary Figure S6C–E maps L-carnitine, L-octanoylcarnitine and octanoyl-CoA, respectively; Supplemental Table 6E retains the paired L-carnitine and octanoyl-CoA node values.

(q–s) Separate cell-level violin distributions in P3 at biopsy, surgery and recurrence show relative inferred pathway activity for (q) Epithelial cytosolic fatty acid activation, (r) Epithelial endoplasmic-reticular fatty acid activation and (s) Myeloid peroxisomal carnitine shuttle. Black dots mark specimen medians; a thin line joins the three medians within each pathway; labels show cell counts. The two Epithelial activation medians rise across the three specimens, whereas the Myeloid shuttle median falls at surgery and returns approximately to its biopsy level at recurrence. Violin widths describe sampled cells, not biological replicates; no cell-level significance test or patient-level recurrence P value is assigned.

(t) Paired biopsy–surgery changes for 12 inferred Human-GEM lipid pathways across six Wu-major cell compartments. Each tile shows the median of seven patient-level log₂(surgery/biopsy) ratios. Red/↑ denotes a higher surgery score and blue/↓ a lower score; the numeral counts patients moving in that direction. Bold symbols indicate six or seven concordant patient pairs. Asterisks mark 23 of 72 tiles with nominal P<0.05 from exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon tests on seven raw patient-median surgery–biopsy pathway-score differences; BH q uses the full 828-test pathway screen, while tile colors summarize paired log₂ ratios. Exact P and screen-wide BH q values are in Supplemental Table 6G. Outlines mark Epithelial fatty-acid activation (L–M) and the Myeloid activation, oxidation and peroxisomal-shuttle pattern (N); horizontal rules separate activation, other fatty-acid routes and remaining lipid pathways. Colors indicate relative inferred score change, not measured lipid abundance or flux. Supplemental Table 6G retains the separate surgery-only cell-type specificity summary across ten pathways.

LC–MS enrichment, node activity and MEIA pathway scores are different analytical quantities. The LC–MS pathway Holm values in b do not transfer to the selected scKNIFE pathways or nodes. The broad pathway and focused carnitine-node screens have no selected result at BH q<0.05. Supplementary Figure S6A–B displays metastatic-status pathway coverage and enrichment as additional tissue context.
