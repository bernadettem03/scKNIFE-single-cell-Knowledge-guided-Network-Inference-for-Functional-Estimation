Figure 4 | A seven-patient TNBC atlas retains broad and fine cellular identities in the H-derived representation.

(a) Sampling and treatment schematic for seven patients (P1–P7). All seven patients contributed biopsy (T0) and surgery (T1) specimens; P3 also contributed a resistant-relapse specimen (T2). The schematic shows six patients in three two-patient display groups and P3 separately; all seven have biopsy–surgery pairs. (b) Specimen-level treatment, procedure, metastatic-status and recorded disease-site annotations. The atlas contains 15 specimens. (c) SingleR major-label predictions against the Wu breast-cancer reference for all 69,391 cells on the saved H-derived UMAP. The eight broad display groups are Epithelial, Endothelial, CAFs, PVL, T-cells, B-cells, Plasmablasts and Myeloid; cancer and normal epithelial reference calls are combined in the Epithelial display label. (d) Five Wu-reference markers per broad group across the same eight groups (40 marker positions). Dot size indicates the percentage of cells with nonzero expression; white-to-red color indicates mean log1p-normalized expression scaled separately for each gene.

(e) Cell-level full-Wu SingleR minor-label predictions on the same H-derived UMAP after default pruning. All 29 reference minor identities are keyed by color; 72 cells have ambiguous calls. Major and minor SingleR tiers were assigned independently. (f) Expression-derived CNV score on the H-derived UMAP; the display scale is clipped at the second and 98th percentiles. (g) Distributions of the same saved CNV scores in the provisional CNV-low/normal (n=27,421) and CNV-high/tumor (n=41,970) groups. CNV scores and status are transcriptome-derived annotations. (h–j) EPCAM, DCN and C1QA expression, respectively, on the H-derived UMAP. (k) Correlations between patient-level epithelial pseudobulk profiles and six TNBC subtype centroids for P1–P7. Supplementary Fig. S4g shows reference-derived marker expression across all 29 minor identities.
