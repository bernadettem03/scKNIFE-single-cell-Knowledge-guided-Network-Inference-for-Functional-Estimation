Figure 4 | A seven-patient TNBC atlas resolves patient-structured epithelial, immune and stromal states.

(B) Correlations between patient-level epithelial pseudobulk profiles and TNBC subtype centroids for seven patients (P1–P7) and the immunomodulatory (IM), basal-like 1 (BL1), mesenchymal stem-like (MSL), mesenchymal (M), basal-like 2 (BL2) and luminal androgen receptor (LAR) classes. Outlined cells mark the assigned subtype; P2 and P6 remained unclassified. (C) Uniform manifold approximation and projection (UMAP) of 69,391 cells from 15 specimens and seven patients, colored by nine differential-expression-based final cell types: epithelial progenitor (n=27,350), cancer-associated fibroblast (n=7,981), T cell (n=7,461), interferon-response epithelial (n=7,082), macrophage (n=7,001), luminal epithelial (n=6,193), endothelial (n=3,374), cycling epithelial (n=2,177) and basal epithelial (n=772). (D) Expression of KRT7, IL2RG, PDGFRB, ELF3, EGFL7, KRT5, KRT14 and MS4A6A on the same embedding. Color limits for each marker span its 2nd to 98th percentile. H labels identify the corresponding scKNIFE H clusters. (E) Expression-derived copy-number variation (CNV) score on the H-graph UMAP and its distribution in cells provisionally classified as normal (n=27,421) or tumor (n=41,970). UMAP colors are limited to the 2nd–98th percentiles. Violin-plot values are cluster-derived scores propagated to member cells and are shown descriptively; cells are not independent genomic measurements, and the classification is not based on DNA copy-number profiling. (F) Heatmap of scaled expression for the top ten ranked genes in each of 12 H clusters across all 69,391 cells. Cells are ordered by H cluster. Annotation strips show patient, site or procedure, metastatic status, library, differential-expression-based cell type and H cluster. Expression values are scaled from −3 to 3.
