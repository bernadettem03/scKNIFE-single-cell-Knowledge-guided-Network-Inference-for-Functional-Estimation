Figure 4 | H-derived TNBC cell atlas with Wu-reference SingleR annotations.

(B) Correlations between patient-level epithelial pseudobulk profiles and TNBC subtype centroids for seven deidentified patients (P1–P7); matrix rows are matched to patient identifiers before display. Outlined cells indicate assigned subtypes; P2 and P6 remained unclassified. (C) Full-atlas SingleR major-label predictions assigned per cell after default pruning, shown for all 69,391 cells on the saved H-derived UMAP. Cancer Epithelial and Normal Epithelial reference calls are collapsed to one broad Epithelial display label (n=36,963); this is not an independent malignancy classification. (D) Cell-level expression of five Wu-reference markers per broad label (40 positions across the same eight broad SingleR groups shown in C), displayed as a dot plot. The combined Epithelial panel uses KRT7, TACSTD2, KRT8, KRT19 and KRT18. Dot size gives the percentage of cells with nonzero expression, and colour gives mean log1p-normalized expression scaled separately for each gene. Repeated markers reflect overlap between reference panels; this plot is descriptive support, not independent validation of the predictions. (E) Existing expression-derived CNV scores on the exact coordinates used in C, and the existing normal/tumor CNV-status distributions (n=27,421 and 41,970 cells). Scores/status were not recomputed. The cell-level violins are descriptive: they are not independent patient-level or DNA-copy-number measurements. (F) Diagnostic epithelial (EPCAM), CAF (DCN), perivascular-like (RGS5), T-cell (CD2), plasmablast (MZB1) and myeloid (C1QA) marker expression on the same saved UMAP. All 69,391 cells are displayed; each log1p(counts per 10,000) marker has its own scale from zero to the 98th percentile of positive cells. These markers support lineage interpretation but do not independently validate every cell-level SingleR call. Marker expression is not exclusive to the named lineage. (G) Cell-level SingleR broad-label relative scores across all 69,391 cells, ordered by predicted broad label and original H-based cluster, with deidentified patient colours (P1–P7). The Epithelial score is the per-cell maximum of the raw Cancer Epithelial and Normal Epithelial scores before recalculating the within-cell range-scaled, cubed display values. These scores are not probabilities or patient-level statistical tests.
