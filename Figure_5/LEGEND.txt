Figure 5 | Aligned scKNIFE H and W factors resolve epithelial knowledge-graph programs and a P3 recurrence-associated glycan–matrix state.

(A) Full decay-selected W-node enrichment for eight node groups (NG1–NG8) derived from consensus clustering of 50 aligned factors. Twenty representative nodes were retained per node group (160 nodes total) and comprise genes, reactions, Gene Ontology terms, metabolites, CellChat entities, metabolic tasks, pathways and gene sets. Values are node-wise z scores of log(1+mean W). Cell-type and cell-state annotation nodes were excluded before clustering and display. (C) W z-score heatmap defining the eight node groups across the 50 factors. Consensus clustering at K=8 produced groups of 6, 3, 2, 28, 1, 3, 2 and 5 factors, respectively (proportion of ambiguous clustering=0.0237; consensus silhouette=0.945). NG5* is the singleton factor-12 group. (E) Network linking node groups (NGs), component clusters (CCs), epithelial cell types and patients. NG-to-CC and CC-to-cell-type edges are signed model-derived associations. Cell-type-to-patient edges represent observed cell counts and are scaled as log10(n+1); the 25 nonzero membership edges sum to all 43,574 epithelial cells. Edge direction and width do not imply cell movement or conserved biological flow. (G) Cell-level heatmap of reconstructed NG activity across 43,574 epithelial cells. Cells are annotated by final epithelial type, procedure or site, metastatic status, patient, treatment, library and cell-type confidence. Activity is displayed as a z score. (H) Distributions of inferred ACGAM2E (N-acetyl-D-glucosamine 2-epimerase) reaction activity and MMP14 node activity among epithelial cells from the longitudinal P3 series: biopsy (Bx; n=137 cells), surgery (n=880) and recurrence (n=4,338). Values are global z scores. Violins summarize cells from one patient and are descriptive; no independent-patient inference was performed. H denotes cell-factor activity, W denotes knowledge-node loading, NG denotes node group and CC denotes component cluster.
