Figure 5 | scKNIFE resolves epithelial programs and a coordinated residual state in P4.

(a) Node-wise z scores of log(1 + mean W) for 180 representative nodes from nine W-derived groups (PG1–PG9). Twenty globally unique nodes per group were selected by exponential decay (tau=5, cumulative mass=0.95); five per group are labelled. Cell-type and cell-state graph nodes were excluded before downstream W clustering and node selection. (b) Network connecting node groups to component clusters and epithelial/state groups through signed model associations. The final layer shows 63 observed epithelial/state-group-to-patient count links totaling 36,963 cells; line widths represent log10(n+1). (c–e) Inferred TAP1 (c), NFKB2 (d), and COL6A1 (e) node activity in P4 (P4), comparing biopsy and surgery after matching by epithelial cell type and RNA depth (434 cells per specimen). P4 had seven recorded cycles of paclitaxel/carboplatin between specimens and subsequently recorded lung and bone metastases. (f–h) Corresponding IFI27 (f), CD74 (g), and APOL6 (h) distributions in P1 (P1; 1,681 matched cells per specimen), who had no recorded metastasis. Violin boxes show medians and quartiles. Node activity is globally standardized log1p(max(H @ W_node, 0)); the cells at the two timepoints are distinct sampled cells. (i) Schematic of P4's paired-sample pattern. Mean matched-cell PG1 activity decreased by 0.809 global z-score units and PG8 increased by 1.320; the fraction classified PG1-low/PG8-high rose from 24% (104/434) at biopsy to 92% (400/434) at surgery using fixed biopsy-median thresholds. Solid arrows indicate established molecular functions; dashed boxes indicate proposed biological consequences. (j) Reconstructed PG1–PG9 activity across all 36,963 epithelial cells, grouped by nine provisional epithelial/state annotations spanning 21 inherited Leiden 0.8 clusters. Annotation tracks show cell type, sample type, metastatic status, patient, and treatment. Panels c–i are descriptive within-patient comparisons; Supplementary Fig. S5j,k shows the all-cell and matched-cell estimator sensitivity.

The L3 marker in panel i refers to the collagen-VI invasion study by Wishart et al. (Science Advances, 2020; doi:10.1126/sciadv.abc3175). Supplementary Fig. S5a–i provides patient coverage, cell-weighted and equal-patient program summaries, PG7 metabolic-task distributions, patient H-factor summaries, network associations, and the P2 MYC/spermidine example. The inherited-cluster-to-cell-state mapping is provided in Figure_5_celltype_key.csv. SingleR terms denote reference annotations; clinical receptor status comes from pathology.
