Figure 7 | Cross-method concordance and patient-resolved PDGF communication in TNBC.

(a) Cell–cell communication analysis from ligand–receptor scores, significance shuffling and downstream network measures. (b) Filtered major-cell-type pathway overlap: 32 shared, 44 CellChat-only and 33 scKNIFE-only pathways. (c) Harmonized ligand–receptor key overlap: 225 shared, 194 CellChat-only and 260 scKNIFE-only keys. (d) Log10 score concordance for 1,917 shared positive ligand–receptor/source/target records (Pearson r=0.732; Spearman ρ=0.746); records are not independent patient replicates. (e) Pooled eight-compartment PDGF communication network; the prominent endothelial-to-PVL connection is highlighted. (f) Complete pooled 29×29 minor-cell PDGF pathway sender–receiver matrix. (g) Endothelial-sender by PVL-receiver slice of the same matrix, with raw scores labelled; Endothelial RGS5→PVL Immature is the largest matrix entry (602.4). (h–k) PDGFB inferred activity, PDGFB expression, PDGFRB inferred activity and PDGFRB expression, respectively, on the saved Figure 4 UMAP. The four displays use independent color scales. (l,m) Endothelial PDGFB and PVL PDGFRB mean log1p expression at biopsy and surgery, respectively, shown as patient-level violins with seven paired specimen values per stage. (n) Absolute major Endothelial→PVL PDGF pathway score shown in the same format; its saved exact paired sign-flip test gives nominal P=0.1094 and BH q=0.6318 across the 2,080-test major screen. Violin width summarizes a smoothed density from seven patient values at each stage; its tapered tails extend beyond observed extrema without crossing zero. Patient-colored dots and connectors preserve the observed pairing, P1 is dotted, and short black bars mark specimen medians. L and M are descriptive expression displays with no separate P value. (o) Ten highest-scoring pooled minor-cell sender→receiver PDGF pathway connections; the Endothelial RGS5→PVL Immature edge ranks first. Panels e–o are descriptive exploration. No comparison in the full 2,080-test major pathway/source/target family passed BH q<0.05. Pooled scores do not establish treatment effects or signaling flux.
