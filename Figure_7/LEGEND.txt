Figure 7 | Orthogonal communication models converge on a VEGF–hypoxia signaling environment among heterogeneous epithelial states.

(A) Workflow integrating CellChat communication probabilities with scKNIFE knowledge-graph associations, followed by pathway and ligand–receptor harmonization, concordance analysis and focused evaluation of VEGF signaling. (B) Overlap of detected signaling pathways: 79 pathways were shared, 8 were CellChat-specific and 171 were scKNIFE-specific (Jaccard index=0.306). (C) Overlap of harmonized ligand–receptor pairs: 551 pairs were shared, 14 were CellChat-specific and 1,710 were scKNIFE-specific (Jaccard index=0.242). (D) Association between 4,405 matched sender–receiver strengths from CellChat and scKNIFE. Axes show log10-transformed strengths; the dashed line indicates a linear fit. Pearson r=0.587 and Spearman rho=0.627 on the transformed scale; raw-scale Pearson r=0.333 and Spearman rho=0.627. Each point represents a matched computational interaction strength. (E) Circular network of the 40 strongest positive VEGF sender-to-receiver edges. Arrows point from sender to receiver; edge widths are scaled between the 2nd and 98th percentiles of weights among the displayed edges. (F) Complete 9×9 matrix of weighted VEGF signaling scores, with rows denoting senders and columns denoting receivers. Colors are clipped at the 98th percentile and matrix values are printed. (G) Eight leading VEGF ligand–receptor pairs ranked by their summed weighted score across 1,134 VEGF interaction records; the two strongest aggregate pairs were VEGFA–KDR (1,090.2) and VEGFA–FLT1 (1,063.2). (H–J) The same H-graph UMAP of 69,391 cells colored by inferred scKNIFE activity for (H) KDR, (I) HALLMARK_HYPOXIA and (J) FLT1. Color limits span the 2nd–98th percentiles for each activity. Values represent inferred activities rather than gene or protein expression. All communication edges are transcriptome-based predictions and do not establish physical interaction or signaling direction in vivo.
