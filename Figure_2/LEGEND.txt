Figure 2 | scKNIFE scales broad knowledge-guided representations while preserving cellular organization and perturbation-linked activity.

(A) Benchmarking workflow comparing scKNIFE with SPECTRA, expiMap and Slalom across breast-cancer single-cell datasets and with conventional Scanpy scoring in the Tahoe perturbation compendium. (B) Implemented prior-knowledge capacity: scKNIFE represented 7,521 distinct biological programs in the benchmark v1 graph and 7,668 in the accepted in-house v2 graph; SPECTRA, expiMap and Slalom used 184, 843 and 118 gene sets or factors, respectively. The 147-program increase in scKNIFE v2 reflects the addition of Human-GEM metabolic pathways. (C) Training runtime in Bassez cohorts 1 and 2, respectively: scKNIFE, 1.44 and 2.13 h; SPECTRA, 12.49 and 13.14 h; expiMap, 14.72 and 15.45 h; Slalom, 7.32 and 11.43 h. Values apply only to the tested hardware, data and settings. (D,F) scKNIFE embeddings for Bassez cohorts 1 and 2, Shiao and Wu TNBC, colored by inferred clusters (D) or author-provided cell types (F). (E) Cluster agreement with author labels by adjusted Rand index, normalized mutual information and adjusted mutual information on shared cells: Bassez cohort 1, n=81,142; cohort 2, n=36,264; Wu TNBC, n=38,251. (G) MAGIC-imputed TP53 expression versus apoptosis activity after doxorubicin in 64,064 cells: scKNIFE Pearson r=0.646, Spearman ρ=0.537; Scanpy r=0.626, ρ=0.398. (H) MAGIC-imputed PIK3R1 expression versus PI3K–AKT–mTOR activity after afatinib in 60,743 cells: scKNIFE r=0.765, ρ=0.703; Scanpy r=0.745, ρ=0.696. Dashed lines are linear fits. Cell-level correlations are descriptive and do not establish patient-level or causal effects.
