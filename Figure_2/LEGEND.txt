Figure 2 | Broad knowledge-guided scKNIFE representations preserve cellular organization and track perturbation-associated programs.

(a) Workflow for breast-cancer single-cell benchmarks and Tahoe cell-line perturbation analysis. (b) Configured biological-program category memberships or comparator gene sets/factors: scKNIFE benchmark v1, 7,521; accepted in-house v2, 7,668; SPECTRA, 184; expiMap, 843; Slalom, 118. These quantities are implementation inputs, not equivalent model units. (c) Recorded training times in Bassez cohorts 1 and 2: scKNIFE, 1.44 and 2.13 h; SPECTRA, 12.49 and 13.14 h; expiMap, 14.72 and 15.45 h; Slalom, 7.32 and 11.43 h. Values apply to the tested data, hardware and settings. (d–g) scKNIFE UMAPs for Bassez cohorts 1 and 2, Shiao and Wu TNBC. (h–k) The corresponding UMAPs colored by author-provided cell-type annotations. (l) Adjusted Rand index (ARI), normalized mutual information (NMI) and adjusted mutual information (AMI) between inferred clusters and author labels for the common-cell intersections of Bassez cohorts 1 and 2 and Wu TNBC. Shiao is not in the four-method agreement comparison; starred SPECTRA entries used cell-type supervision. (m,n) MAGIC-imputed PIK3R1 expression versus PI3K–AKT–mTOR scores after afatinib in 60,743 Tahoe cells, using scKNIFE (m) and Scanpy (n). (o,p) MAGIC-imputed TP53 expression versus apoptosis scores after doxorubicin in 64,064 cells, using scKNIFE (o) and Scanpy (p). Printed Pearson r and Spearman ρ values are cell-level descriptive correlations; they do not establish patient-level or causal effects.
