Supplementary Figure S1 | Pooled W-space diagnostics show shared structure and cohort-specific variation across scKNIFE representations.

(A) Cross-dataset linear centered-kernel alignment (CKA) for independently normalized W matrices from Bassez cohort 1, Bassez cohort 2, Shiao and Wu. Pairwise CKA values were 0.895 (Bassez 1–Bassez 2), 0.669 (Bassez 1–Shiao), 0.826 (Bassez 1–Wu), 0.691 (Bassez 2–Shiao), 0.807 (Bassez 2–Wu) and 0.719 (Shiao–Wu). The accompanying table reports the number of graph nodes, factors, effective rank and its fraction of the factor count, median and 95th-percentile absolute factor correlation, and condition number. Bassez cohort 1 contained 22,951 nodes and 40 factors (effective rank 22.21, 55.5%; median |r|=0.167; 95th percentile |r|=0.467; condition number=10.23); cohort 2 contained 40 factors (effective rank 22.90, 57.3%; 0.148; 0.466; 8.90); Shiao contained 80 factors (56.80, 71.0%; 0.034; 0.283; 7.35); and Wu contained 30 factors (15.78, 52.6%; 0.239; 0.551; 7.87). (B) t-distributed stochastic neighbor embedding (t-SNE) of pooled, independently normalized W blocks, colored by knowledge-node type. (C) The same pooled W embedding colored by unsupervised pooled clusters. Embeddings and diagnostics use frozen W-derived assets and are descriptive; no inferential test was applied.
