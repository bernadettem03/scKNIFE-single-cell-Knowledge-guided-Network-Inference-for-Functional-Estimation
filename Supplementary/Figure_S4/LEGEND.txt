Supplementary Figure S4 | Complementary composition, marker and CNV views support annotation of the seven-patient TNBC atlas.

(a) Stacked percentage composition of the 12 original annotation classes across 15 patient–site or procedure specimens. Each bar sums to 100%. (b) Overall percentage composition of the 12 original classes across 69,391 cells. (c) Canonical-marker dot plot showing 59 genes across the 12 original annotation classes. Dot size denotes the fraction of cells expressing each marker, and color identifies the annotated cell type. (d) UMAP of all 69,391 cells colored by the 12 original annotation classes. (e) CNV-QC UMAP colored by 26 expression-derived CNV Leiden clusters. (f) The same CNV-QC UMAP colored by cell type. (g) The same embedding colored by provisional expression-derived CNV status: high/tumor (n=41,970) or low/normal (n=27,421). CNV status is a transcriptome-derived annotation and not an independent DNA-based copy-number measurement. Panels are listed in their displayed order.
