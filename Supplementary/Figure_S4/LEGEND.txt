Supplementary Figure S4 | Complementary composition, marker and CNV views support annotation of the seven-patient TNBC atlas.

(a) Stacked percentage composition of the 12 original annotation classes across 15 patient–site or procedure specimens. Each bar sums to 100%. (b) Overall percentage composition of the 12 original classes across 69,391 cells. (c) Canonical-marker dot plot showing 59 genes across the 12 original annotation classes. Dot size denotes the fraction of cells expressing each marker, and color identifies the annotated cell type. (d) UMAP of all 69,391 cells colored by the 12 original annotation classes. (e) CNV-QC UMAP colored by 26 expression-derived CNV Leiden clusters. (f) The provisional embedding colored by expression-derived CNV status: high/tumor (n=41,970) or low/normal (n=27,421). CNV status is a transcriptome-derived annotation and not an independent DNA-based copy-number measurement. (g) Full-page white-to-red dot plot across all 29 pruned full-Wu SingleR minor identities, grouped by Wu major lineage. Two markers per identity (58 displayed positions; shared genes may recur) were selected from the saved Wu reference-derived pairwise marker sets: the most recurrent marker and a second marker with the highest within-lineage expression contrast weighted by detection in the query. Dot size indicates the percentage of cells with expression >0; color indicates mean log1p-normalized expression, including zeros, min–max scaled separately for each displayed gene. Cell counts appear beside each identity. Panel g is descriptive expression support for the reference predictions. Panels are listed in their displayed order across two pages.
