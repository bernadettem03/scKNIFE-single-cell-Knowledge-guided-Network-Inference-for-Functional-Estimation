Supplementary Figure S5 | Sensitivity and supporting analyses of epithelial node-group structure.

(B) Sensitivity of consensus solutions at K=6 and K=8. The proportion of ambiguous clustering (PAC; lower indicates less ambiguous consensus assignment), 1−consensus silhouette (lower indicates greater separation) and factor group sizes are shown for each solution. K=8 was used for the main interpretive analysis. (D) Patient representation and epithelial node-group activity summarized using cell-weighted and equal-patient estimators. Patient representation is log10(cells+1). Activity heatmaps compare how unequal patient contribution affects cell-type–node-group patterns. (E) Cell-level distributions for five exemplar non-gene node scores across epithelial types: folate transmembrane transport in progenitor cells, hydrogen sulfide metabolism in luminal cells, basement-membrane assembly in basal cells, DNA replication with RNA-primer synthesis in cycling cells and HALLMARK_INTERFERON_GAMMA_RESPONSE in interferon-response epithelial cells. Cell counts were 27,350 progenitor, 6,193 luminal, 772 basal, 2,177 cycling and 7,082 interferon-response epithelial cells. Distributions are descriptive and do not constitute patient-level tests. (F) Patient-average epithelial program activity for P1–P7 and NG1–NG8. Values are cell-weighted patient means and are z-scored within each node-group column; the panel is descriptive. (G) Association diagram linking node groups to component clusters and epithelial cell types. Edges encode model-derived associations and do not represent cell transitions or physical signaling.
