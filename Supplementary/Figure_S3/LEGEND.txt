Supplementary Figure S3A | Patient-level statistical audit of external-cohort metabolic programs.

(S3A) Patient-level statistical audit. Candidate-ranking dot plots summarize screened associations for clinical responder versus non-responder groups and T-cell clonotype-expanded versus non-expanded groups. Selected Shiao patient-median comparisons show (C) myeloid N-glycan processing in the endoplasmic reticulum (R, n=9; NR, n=4; NR-minus-R Cliff’s delta=−0.778; exact two-sided permutation P=0.0951; q=1.000), (D) tumor or epithelial phosphatidylinositol synthesis (R, n=10; NR, n=6; delta=0.667; P=0.0321; q=1.000) and (E) T-cell glycogen biosynthesis (R, n=10; NR, n=5; delta=0.640; P=0.208; q=1.000). Selected Bassez comparisons across cohorts A and B show (F) myeloid glycogen degradation [cohort A: E, n=5; NE, n=7; NE-minus-E delta=−0.486; P=0.0909; q=0.672; cohort B: E, n=2; NE, n=2; delta=−0.500; P=0.667; q=1.000], (G) tumor or epithelial gluconeogenesis from lactate [cohort A: E, n=5; NE, n=7; delta=0.829; P=0.0126; q=0.595; cohort B: E, n=3; NE, n=2; delta=0.667; P=0.400; q=1.000] and (H) T-cell endoplasmic-reticulum-associated degradation [cohort A: E, n=5; NE, n=7; delta=0.486; P=0.495; q=0.799; cohort B: E, n=3; NE, n=3; delta=0.333; P=0.600; q=1.000]. Points represent patient medians; 95% confidence intervals were obtained by bootstrap resampling, P values by exact two-sided permutation tests and q values by Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the audited comparisons.


Supplementary Figure S3B | Pooled-cell metabolic-task and Gene Ontology summaries.

(S3B) Metabolic-task and Gene Ontology heatmaps for Bassez cohorts A and B, stratified by E and NE groups. Gene Ontology panels show the 25 globally Benjamini–Hochberg-significant terms with the greatest mean log2 fold change separately for E and NE. Values are unscaled log2 fold changes and rows are clustered independently within each cohort. Bassez cohort B NE B cells were not tested because only 15 pretreatment cells were available, below the prespecified minimum of 20. These pooled-cell summaries are descriptive because cells are nested within patients.


Supplementary Figure S3C | Embedding localization of baseline and longitudinal metabolic programs.

(S3C) Baseline and longitudinal activities localized on frozen cohort embeddings. Baseline maps show arachidonate synthesis, endoplasmic-reticulum-associated degradation, myeloid glycogen degradation and T-cell endoplasmic-reticulum-associated degradation in the relevant cohorts and cell types. Longitudinal rows show group-by-time activity maps and pooled categorical maps for the four analyses reported in Figure 3G–J. Scores are log10(scKNIFE activity+1×10^−10), with common clipping within each association. Only cells from eligible patients and the indicated cell type are displayed. Maps are descriptive and do not alter the patient-level unit of inference. R, responder; NR, non-responder; E, clonotype expanded; NE, clonotype non-expanded.
