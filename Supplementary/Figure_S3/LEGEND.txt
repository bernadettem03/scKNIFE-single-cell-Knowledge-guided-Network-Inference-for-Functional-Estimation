Supplementary Figure 3A | Candidate concordance and Bassez differential-activity summaries.

Publication layout: one A4 landscape page places A–D across the top row and E–F below, with a matching PNG. Main figure text is 6 pt Carlito (Calibri-compatible); dense heatmap and GO labels are smaller.

(A) Patient directional concordance for the leading clinical R/NR baseline candidates across Myeloid, Tumor/epithelial and T-cell compartments; outlined points mark selected candidates, including the tumor/epithelial phosphatidyl-inositol-synthesis task displayed in main Figure 3E. (B) Patient directional concordance for leading Bassez E/NE baseline candidates; outlined points mark selected candidates and E/NE denotes clonotype expansion. (C–D) Patient-level GO treatment-response interaction forest plots for Bassez cohorts A and B, respectively. Within each cell type, GO activity was summarized by the patient median at Pre and On among patients with at least 20 cells at both timepoints, and the paired response was log2(On/Pre). Points and horizontal bars show the NE-minus-E interaction estimate from a limma model fitted to patient-level responses and its 95% confidence interval. An asterisk indicates global BH q<0.05 across all testable GO-by-cell-type interactions within that cohort. Open diamonds show descriptive median effects when either group has fewer than three paired patients (`NT`); `NA` marks an absent group. Bassez A was used to select three nonredundant displayed terms per cell type by global BH q followed by absolute effect, and the same terms were displayed in Bassez B. Display redundancy filtering did not change the tested universe or multiple-testing correction. (E–F) Pooled-cell On-versus-Pre log2 fold-change heatmaps for metabolic-task activity in Bassez cohorts A and B, respectively. Each heatmap displays the top 10 E and top 10 NE tasks ranked by mean significant log2 fold change, plus glutathione synthesis and clathrin-dependent endosomal trafficking where available; rows and columns are clustered within the displayed subset. Column labels show the full cell type and E/NE group; the source label `Cancer_cell` is displayed as Tumor/epithelial. These heatmaps are descriptive because cells are nested within patients. GO and metabolic-task values are inferred activities and do not establish pathway flux or causality.

Supplementary Figure 3B | Frozen-embedding localization of the activities displayed in main Figure 3E–J.

The seven maps are arranged on one A4 landscape page with 6 pt Carlito text.

(A) Shiao baseline Tumor/epithelial phosphatidyl-inositol synthesis corresponding to main E. (B,C) Baseline Tumor/epithelial gluconeogenesis from lactate in Bassez cohorts A and B, corresponding to main F and its cohort replication. (D) Bassez A On-treatment Myeloid glutathione synthesis corresponding to main G. (E) Bassez A On-treatment B-cell clathrin-dependent endosomal trafficking corresponding to main H. (F) Shiao RTPD1 Fibroblast alanine synthesis from glutamine corresponding to main I. (G) Shiao PD1 Tumor/epithelial bilirubin synthesis corresponding to main J. Grey points show all endpoint-annotated cells at the displayed timepoint, and coloured target cells show log10-transformed inferred task activity. Outcome groups are pooled in the G–J treated-timepoint maps. Coordinates are frozen cohort-specific UMAP embeddings; colours should be compared only within an association.
