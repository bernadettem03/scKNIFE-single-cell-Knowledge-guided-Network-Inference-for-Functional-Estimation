Figure 3 | External breast-cancer cohorts reveal method-concordant and patient-variable metabolic programs associated with treatment and outcome groups.

(a–d) UpSet plots comparing significant cell-type–metabolic-task associations identified by scKNIFE and scCellFie in (a) clonotype-expanded (e) and (b) non-expanded (NE) groups from Bassez cohort B and in (c) responders (R) and (d) non-responders (NR) from the Shiao cohort. Totals for scKNIFE only/shared/scCellFie only were 374/766/109 in Bassez E, 289/533/176 in Bassez NE, 439/863/115 in Shiao R and 267/984/38 in Shiao NR. Shared denotes task–cell-type membership irrespective of effect direction; same-direction counts were 613/766, 343/533, 494/863 and 754/984, respectively (57–80%). (e) Baseline tumor/epithelial phosphatidyl-inositol-synthesis activity in Shiao responders (n=10 patients) and non-responders (n=6 patients). The NR-minus-R Cliff’s delta was 0.667 (95% bootstrap confidence interval, 0.200 to 1.000; exact two-sided permutation P=0.0321; Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted q=1.000), with 15 of 16 patients concordant with the group direction. (f) Baseline tumor/epithelial gluconeogenesis-from-lactate activity in Bassez clonotype-expanded and non-expanded groups. In cohort A, n=5 E and n=7 NE patients (NE-minus-E Cliff’s delta=0.829; 95% confidence interval, 0.314 to 1.000; P=0.0126; q=0.595; 12/12 patients directionally concordant); in cohort B, n=3 E and n=2 NE patients (delta=0.667; 95% confidence interval, −0.333 to 1.000; P=0.400; q=1.000; 4/5 concordant). Boxes in e and f show the median and interquartile range, and points represent patient-level medians. P values are from exact two-sided permutation tests; confidence intervals were obtained by bootstrap resampling and q values by Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the audited comparisons. e and f are ranked exploratory associations; neither survives patient-level multiple-testing correction. (g–j) Treatment-minus-baseline changes in patient-median metabolic-task activity. Each point is one paired patient with at least 20 cells of the indicated type at both timepoints; boxes show the median and interquartile range, and the dashed line marks zero change. (g) Myeloid glutathione-synthesis activity in Bassez cohort A: E, n=5, median log2 change=0.812; NE, n=7, median=−0.700; NE-minus-E Cliff’s delta=−0.829; exact two-sided Mann–Whitney P=0.0177. (h) B-cell clathrin-dependent endosomal-trafficking activity in Bassez cohort A: E, n=5, median=0.510 (5/5 changing in the median direction); NE, n=4, median=−0.075 (3/4 concordant); delta=−1.000; P=0.0159. This task was nominally significant but not significant after correction across all 1,308 Bassez A cell-type–task longitudinal tests (BH q=0.346), and is therefore exploratory. (i) Fibroblast alanine synthesis from glutamine between baseline and RTPD1 in Shiao responders (n=8; median=−0.375) and non-responders (n=4; median=0.757), with NR-minus-R delta=0.750 and P=0.0485. (j) Tumor-cell bilirubin synthesis between baseline and PD1 in Shiao responders (n=10; median=0.126) and non-responders (n=6; median=0.480), with delta=0.633 and P=0.0420. P values compare patient-level changes using exact two-sided Mann–Whitney tests. E and NE denote T-cell clonotype-expanded and non-expanded groups, respectively, and do not denote clinical response; R and NR denote clinical responder and non-responder. The clathrin-trafficking task is a broad inferred activity and does not establish antigen presentation, metabolic flux or causality.
