Figure 3 | External breast-cancer cohorts reveal method-concordant and patient-variable metabolic programs associated with treatment and outcome groups.

(A–D) UpSet plots comparing significant cell-type–metabolic-task associations identified by scKNIFE and scCellFie in (A) clonotype-expanded (E) and (B) non-expanded (NE) groups from Bassez cohort B and in (C) responders (R) and (D) non-responders (NR) from the Shiao cohort. Totals for scKNIFE only/shared/scCellFie only were 374/766/109 in Bassez E, 289/533/176 in Bassez NE, 439/863/115 in Shiao R and 267/984/38 in Shiao NR. (E) Baseline T-cell activity for recognition of misfolded protein, an endoplasmic-reticulum-associated degradation task, in Shiao responders (n=10 patients) and non-responders (n=5 patients). The NR-minus-R Cliff’s delta was 0.480 (95% bootstrap confidence interval, −0.200 to 1.000; exact two-sided permutation P=0.0926; Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted q=1.000). (F) Baseline myeloid-cell glycogen-degradation activity in Bassez clonotype-expanded and non-expanded groups. In cohort A, n=5 E and n=7 NE patients (NE-minus-E Cliff’s delta=−0.486; 95% confidence interval, −1.000 to 0.257; P=0.0909; q=0.672); in cohort B, n=2 E and n=2 NE patients (delta=−0.500; 95% confidence interval, −1.000 to 1.000; P=0.667; q=1.000). Boxes in E and F show the median and interquartile range, and points represent patient-level medians. P values are from exact two-sided permutation tests; confidence intervals were obtained by bootstrap resampling and q values by Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the audited comparisons. (G–J) Paired patient-level changes in metabolic-task activity between baseline and on-treatment samples. Patients were included when at least 20 cells of the indicated type were available at both timepoints. (G) Myeloid glutathione-synthesis activity in Bassez cohort A, comparing E (n=5; median log2 change=0.812) and NE (n=7; median=−0.700) patients (exact two-sided Mann–Whitney P=0.0177). (H) T-cell glutamine-linked activity in Bassez cohort B, comparing E (n=3; median=0.317) and NE (n=3; median=−0.275) patients (P=0.100). (I) Fibroblast alanine synthesis from glutamine between baseline and RTPD1 in Shiao responders (n=8; median=−0.375) and non-responders (n=4; median=0.757; P=0.0485). (J) Tumor-cell bilirubin synthesis between baseline and PD1 in Shiao responders (n=10; median=0.126) and non-responders (n=6; median=0.480; P=0.0420). Lines join measurements from the same patient; between-group P values compare patient-level changes using exact two-sided Mann–Whitney tests. Box-plot coordinates display normalized activity (%) on a log2 scale. Brackets above the cell-level distributions report Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted pooled-cell Wilcoxon q values for descriptive localization only; cells are not treated as independent biological replicates. E and NE denote T-cell clonotype-expanded and non-expanded groups, respectively, and do not denote clinical response; R and NR denote clinical responder and non-responder.
